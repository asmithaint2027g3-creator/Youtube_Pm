## Key Production Takeaways

1. **Mandatory Disclaimer Integration:**
   - Both the $17.18M Cost of Inaction model (Video 1) and the CFO DCF Financial Model (Video 3) have prominent, high-contrast, on-screen text disclaimers running continuously through their respective scenes, fully synchronized with spoken narration disclaimers.
2. **Third-Party Trademark Protection:**
   - Where third-party software tools (Salesforce, NetSuite, HubSpot, Gong, etc.) are discussed, visuals utilize clean stylized category icons and typographic labels to respect trademark guidelines and prevent any false claims of formal corporate partnership.
3. **No Fictional Customers or Testimonials:**
   - All scenario walk-throughs (e.g., cold-storage pharmaceutical firm, $60M SaaS enterprise) are presented as composite architectural case patterns, with zero invented customer logos or fabricated executive quotes.
