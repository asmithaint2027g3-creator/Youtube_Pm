# ProfitMachines Executive Video Series: Outlines & Production Framework

## 1. Series Overview

This repository holds the strategic outlines and production planning for the foundational 3-part executive YouTube video series for **ProfitMachines**, the AI-Native Revenue Transformation Platform.

The series is directly derived from ProfitMachines' verified core positioning, live website architecture, and the executive research publication:  
**“From Revenue Friction to Profitable Growth: An Executive Guide to AI-Native Revenue Transformation”**.

The primary objective is to educate B2B enterprise decision-makers (CEOs, CROs, CFOs, CMOs, and RevOps leaders) on moving away from brute-force headcount scaling and point-tool sprawl, and toward an empirical, mathematically auditable, AI-native revenue engine.

## 4. Non-Negotiable Editorial Guardrails

1. **Grounded in Verified Data:** Every benchmark, formula, and concept is strictly tied to verified sources (Gartner, Salesforce, Mark Roberge's Sales Velocity Equation, SPICED, MEDDPICC, and the ProfitMachines baseline financial model).
2. **Zero Hallucination Policy:** No fictional customer logos, invented testimonials, unverified certifications, or fake revenue guarantees are included.
3. **Executive Tone:** Authoritative, empirical, consultative, and devoid of hyperbolic influencer or generic tech-bro buzzwords.
4. **Current Phase:** **Outline & Structural Planning Only.** Full scripting, voice-overs, and video asset generation will commence strictly upon user approval.
